About Saspolo Hotel

A hotel that feels like Skardu itself

Family-run, in the heart of town, and shaped by years of welcoming travelers.

Some places are built with bricks and stone. Others are built from memories.

Nestled in the breathtaking mountains of Skardu, Saspolo was born from a simple dream: to create a place where every traveler could feel at home, even miles away from their own.

Here, the mountains tell stories, the skies feel closer, and every stay becomes a memory. Whether you come seeking adventure, peace, or a break from the noise of everyday life, we hope you leave with more than photographs; we hope you leave with a feeling of home.

Welcome to Saspolo, where every journey becomes a story.
